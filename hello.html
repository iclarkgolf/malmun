Quick quiz: 3 questions

No sign up. Tap an answer.

1.You leave your friend's house. Your friend's mom says "들어가세요." What does she mean?
It sounds like "go in," but here it is a warm goodbye. It means "Go into your home safely."
2.Your friend says "설마 비 오겠어?" and goes out without an umbrella. Heavy rain comes. What does mom say?
설마가 사람 잡는다 means "surely not" catches people. Mom is saying, "See? I told you."
3.A Korean coworker asks you "밥 먹었어요?" What is the best answer?
"Have you eaten?" is often just "Hi, how are you?" A short answer and the same question back is perfect.
